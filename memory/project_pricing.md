Pricing is Kenya-first (KES), not global USD pricing. Decided 2026-05-05:
- **Starter (learner tier)**: KES 299/month or KES 150/2 weeks (bi-weekly option)
- **Pro**: KES 799/month (no bi-weekly)

**Why:** Kenya market; USD prices feel low-value globally but are appropriate locally. Bi-weekly option lowers commitment barrier for students. Big gap between Starter and Pro (500 KES) is intentional — Pro must deliver a noticeably better experience.

**How to apply:** Always use KES as primary currency display. Pro tier features must meaningfully exceed Starter (unlimited assessments, premium AI model, analytics, study groups, priority support).
