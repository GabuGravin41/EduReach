# EduReach Pricing Strategy — Founder Brief

**Date:** May 2026  
**Status:** Proposed — pending co-founder review

---

## Recommended Tier Structure (3 Tiers)

| Tier | Monthly KES | Monthly USD | Annual KES | Annual USD |
|------|------------|------------|------------|------------|
| **Free** | KES 0 | $0 | — | — |
| **Starter** | KES 399 | $2.99 | KES 3,499 (~2 months free) | $29.99 |
| **Pro** | KES 999 | $7.99 | KES 9,999 (~2 months free) | $79.99 |

**New user onboarding:** All signups automatically receive a **14-day free Pro trial** — no payment required.

---

## What Each Tier Gets

### Free
- 15 AI Tutor queries / month
- Create up to 3 assessments
- Create up to 2 courses
- Browse all public courses
- Community access

### Starter — KES 399/month
- 100 AI Tutor queries / month
- Create up to 15 assessments
- Create up to 5 courses
- Access all public courses
- AI quiz generation

### Pro — KES 999/month
- 500 AI Tutor queries / month
- Unlimited assessments & courses
- Premium AI model (Gemini 2.5 Pro)
- Analytics dashboard
- Study groups
- Private courses & exams
- "Pro" badge on profile

---

## Why 3 Tiers, Not 4

We previously had a "Pro Plus" tier (KES 1,700/month). Removing it because:

1. **Too many choices reduces conversion.** Studies show 3-tier pricing converts better than 4. The middle tier (Starter) anchors value; the top tier (Pro) captures serious users.
2. **Pro Plus had no clear buyer persona.** Who buys it over Pro? Without a clear answer, it confuses more than it sells.
3. **We can reintroduce it as an "Institution/School" plan** when we have actual schools asking — and close those deals manually at custom pricing.

---

## Why We Give Free Users 15 AI Queries (Not Zero)

The AI Tutor is our best feature. If free users never experience it, they have no reason to pay for more.

- **15 queries = ~3 meaningful tutoring sessions per month.** Enough to get real value and feel the limit.
- **Not so many that paid users feel cheated.** 100 (Starter) is 6× more; 500 (Pro) is 33× more.
- **Competitor comparison:**
  - Khan Academy AI: free but limited, no paywall
  - Chegg: $15.95/month, no free tier
  - Coursera: free audit, $49/month for certificates
  - Our KES 399 ($2.99) is positioned well below global edtech norms for a full-featured AI tutor

---

## Why KES 399 for Starter (Not KES 249 or KES 499)

| Price | Risk |
|-------|------|
| KES 249 | Too cheap — signals low quality; margins thin |
| KES 349 | Slightly awkward number; not meaningfully different from 399 |
| **KES 399** | Psychological sweet spot — feels under KES 400, is M-Pesa-friendly |
| KES 499 | Starts to feel like a commitment for students |

M-Pesa payments work best at round-ish numbers. KES 399 is clean.

---

## Why 14-Day Trial (Not 7-Day)

- 7 days is not enough to build a habit. Users sign up, get busy, and the trial expires before they've had 3 sessions.
- 14 days spans two full school weeks — users experience at least one full study cycle (assignments, revision, exam prep).
- **Risk:** Some users abuse trials. Mitigation: one trial per account, ever (already enforced in our system).

## Comparison With Alternative Models

### Option A: Freemium only (no paid tiers)
- **Pro:** Maximum user growth
- **Con:** No revenue. Needs VC funding or grants to sustain. Not viable for a bootstrapped team.

### Option B: Paywall everything (no free tier)
- **Pro:** Higher average revenue per user
- **Con:** Zero trust-building, especially in Kenya where students research before paying. Churn is high.

### Option C: Per-use credits (pay per AI query)
- **Pro:** Feels fair
- **Con:** Complex UX, anxiety about spending, no predictable revenue. Hard to build habits.

### Option D: Our model — Freemium + Subscription + Trial ✓
- **Pro:** Free tier builds habits → trial converts skeptics → subscription retains. Predictable monthly revenue.
- **Con:** Some users stay on free forever. Acceptable — they still bring word-of-mouth.

---

## Annual Plans — Why Push Hard

1. **Cash flow:** Annual payment = 10–12 months of revenue upfront.
2. **Retention:** Users who paid annually don't churn monthly — they stay to get value.
3. **Kenyan school calendar:** Parents and teachers budget per school year (Jan and Sept). Annual plans align with this.

Suggested annual discount: **2 months free** (pay 10, get 12). Simple to communicate, easy to calculate.
